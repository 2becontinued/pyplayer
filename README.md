I got mad that apple music has a horrible interface, so I wrote my own terminal
music player.

It currently comes half-made, your albums and playlists are to be
added in via a new .py file, dragging and dropping your songs and
making sure its imported and whatnot.

Currently, theres little features, but I plan for there to be a little
animation of a record players tonearm moving to the start of a record and lowering
in ascii.
